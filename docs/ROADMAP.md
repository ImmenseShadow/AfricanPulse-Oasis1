# Roadmap

* **Done:** Phase 1 news collection and Streamlit dashboard. Phase 2 conversion to a Flask website with source management, activity history and settings.
* **Done (Increment 2):** Google Alerts RSS feeds as a source type, with safe URL handling, publisher and topic, multi source references for deduplicated articles and activity records per source.
* **Next:** better categorisation rules, tags, export of articles marked Selected for Content, optional scheduled collection.
* **Possible future enhancement:** collecting Google Alerts delivered by email (needs mailbox access and authentication; deliberately not part of Increment 2).
* **Later:** African culture and history, holidays and festivals, country information (each with sources and country or community context), optional AI assisted drafting that is clearly labelled and reviewed by a person, authentication if the site is ever shared on a network, production deployment.

Not implemented and not stubbed: cultural databases, holiday calendars, AI summaries, social media publishing, multi user features.
